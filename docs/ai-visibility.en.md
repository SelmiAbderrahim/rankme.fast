# AI Visibility

Many buyers now ask an AI assistant instead of scanning a page of search results, so the answer they get is often the first thing they see. AI Visibility tracks whether those answers mention your brand, point people to your site, or send them to a competitor. It is an **Agency** feature.

## Tracked prompts

A prompt is a question a buyer might ask an AI assistant, for example "best project management tool for a small team". You add the prompts that matter to your business. You can track up to **10 prompts per site**. Pick the ones closest to a buying decision; that is where being mentioned is worth the most.

## Running a check

A check runs each tracked prompt and reads the answer the assistant gives back. For every prompt it records:

- whether your brand is **mentioned** by name,
- whether your site is **linked** as a source,
- which **competitors** are named,
- whether the mention is **positive, neutral, or negative**.

"Mentioned" means your brand name appears in the answer. Being linked as a source is tracked separately and is the stronger signal.

## Share of voice

Across all your tracked prompts, share of voice is the share of answers that mention you at all. Rising share of voice means you are showing up in more of the answers buyers see; a competitor climbing while you stay flat is an early warning.

## How to read the results

This is **opportunity tracking, not a ranking guarantee**. AI answers are generated fresh each time and can change between runs, people, and regions, so the same prompt can mention you today and not tomorrow. Watch the trend across many checks rather than any single result. No one can promise you a place in an AI answer.

## Limits

Tracked prompts are capped at 10 per site, and each account has a monthly allowance of checks. When the allowance runs out, checks pause until the next monthly reset. Your history stays visible. See [Plans, limits & credits](./plans-limits-credits.en.md) for the numbers.
