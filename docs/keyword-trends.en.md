# Keyword Trends

Keyword Trends shows how search interest changes over time. For up to five keywords at once, it reports a relative interest index, year-over-year direction, recent momentum and seasonal months.

## What one unit covers

Each exploration you confirm uses one `trend_explorations` unit, whether it has one keyword or five. Previewing, cancelling a preview, reopening a saved run or picking a related-query follow-up costs nothing until you confirm. A confirmed follow-up counts as a new exploration.

See [Pricing](./pricing.en.md) for current allowances and pack prices.

## The index is an estimate

Every series and readout is labeled **Estimate**. The 0–100 interest index is relative to the market and time window you chose. It isn't monthly search volume, traffic or a forecast.

- Year-over-year needs at least 56 valid data points.
- Momentum needs 12 data points and shows up, down or flat.
- Seasonality needs 24 separate months. If there isn't enough history, RankMeFast tells you instead of making up a pattern.

A flat, sparse or empty response that succeeded is still a real result, not an error.

## Cache and refunds

A cached result still uses one unit, and so does a successful exploration that returns no series.

If the source fails before any series is saved, the reserved unit is returned once and the run is marked refunded. Once a usable series is saved, the unit stays spent even if another readout isn't available.

## When to explore

Keyword Trends runs **on demand**; it doesn't monitor keywords continuously. Run it again when you want a newer view. If new explorations are paused, your saved runs stay readable.

Open **Live trends** from Keyword Research, enter up to five phrases, choose the market and language, check the spend preview and confirm. For monthly caps and the general refund rule, see [Plans, limits & credits](./plans-limits-credits.en.md).
