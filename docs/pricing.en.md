# Pricing

RankMeFast sells you a monthly allowance of SEO checks. Starter and Pro suit founders and in-house teams; Agency is built for client work.

## Fixed or custom

The published plans below have fixed prices and limits. With the custom builder you pick your own mix of monthly allowances, sites and seats, features, and rank-check frequency, and the server prices it. Its yearly discount is worked out from the matching monthly quote, so it can be lower than 20%, zero, or not offered when the price would fall below our cost floor. Quotes last 30 minutes. Checkout appears only for an owner with a verified email, and only when payments and pricing data are ready. Polar adds taxes at checkout. See [Plans, limits & credits](./plans-limits-credits.en.md) for resets, renewals, plan changes, packs, cancellation, and refunds.

## What we count

Every plan meters the same units:

- **Audit**: one full audit run over a site.
- **Audit page**: one page inside that run (crawl budget).
- **Tracked keyword**: one keyword you keep watching.
- **Rank check**: one SERP fetch for a tracked keyword; the weekly sweep and every manual re-check count.
- **Keyword lookup**: one site-keyword discovery request or one read from the full keyword-research panel. Discovery answered from your own Search Console data costs nothing.
- **Backlink row**: one row on the backlink list.
- **AI summary:** one plain-language recap of the fix-now list from the configured AI provider.
- **Competitor lookup**: one competitor list or intersection call.

Cached results still count as one check. Caching keeps our vendor costs, and your price, down.

Monthly keyword lookups are **20 Starter / 50 Pro / 100 Agency**. Site discovery is included on every plan; full keyword research remains Pro and Agency.

Competitor Intelligence starts on Pro. A landscape uses one keyword lookup for each selected competitor, up to three on Pro and ten on Agency. An optional competitor discovery refresh uses one more keyword lookup after a separate confirmation. Agency ranking-page content comparison uses the separate `competitor_content_runs` allowance. [Read the competitor guide](./backlinks-competitors.en.md).

## New intelligence units

<!-- source: tiers.ts -->
- **Keyword Trends exploration**: one confirmed exploration of up to five phrases.
- **Traffic Insights snapshot**: one confirmed target domain.
- **Link Intelligence check**: one deep pull, or one unit for each competitor in a link-gap run.
- **Review Intelligence sync**: one whole sync of one to three configured sources.
- **Brand Radar scan**: one brand query; its cited digest is included.

These five features run when you start them; they don't monitor continuously. Cached results count, and so do searches that succeed but find nothing. You get a unit back (once) only if the data source fails before the feature has saved anything useful. Each guide explains how this works for jobs with several parts. Brand Radar always fetches fresh results for the site you started it from and never shares mention data between accounts.

## What happens at a cap

1. The app warns you before you hit a cap.
2. New requests hard-stop at the cap. Nothing is billed beyond the ceiling.
3. You then have three options: wait for the monthly reset, buy a credit pack (+80 keyword lookups for $19, or +5 audits for $29; one-time packs that never expire), or upgrade your plan.

The keyword pack is the `keywords-1k` pack: +80 `keyword_lookups` for $19, one-time. It tops up keyword gap, overview, and trends reads when your monthly allowance runs out. Audience Research runs have no credit pack, so upgrade or wait for the reset.

## Yearly billing

Published fixed plans give you two months free, which is 20% off 12 monthly payments. A custom plan shows its effective discount, which may be smaller or unavailable. You can request an interval change from billing, but your paid period stays unchanged and the new interval starts only after a fresh checkout at the period boundary.

## Cancelling

Cancel from the billing page. You don't need to call anyone. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the paid period. Your audits and rank history then remain read-only for 30 days. Refund requests follow the terms shown at checkout.

## Enterprise plans

Larger organizations can agree their own limits, price, and seat count with us instead of picking a published plan. There's no self-serve enterprise signup: [tell us what you need](/enterprise) and we'll put an agreement together. See [Enterprise plans](./enterprise.en.md).
