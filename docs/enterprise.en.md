# Enterprise plans

A published plan works for most organizations. If you need higher usage limits, more seats, or billing that fits your procurement process, we can agree an **enterprise plan** with you directly.

There's no self-serve enterprise signup. You tell us what you need, we work out an agreement, and we switch it on for your account.

## How to start

Fill in the form on the [enterprise page](/enterprise). Tell us your organization, roughly how much you expect to use each month, and how many people need access. We will come back to you to work through the details.

## What an agreement sets

An agreement records four things for your organization.

<!-- generated: finite-enterprise-limits:start -->
**Usage limits.** Enterprise starts with 100 live sites and the baseline limits shown in the plan catalog. Every override you save is a set number, never unlimited. Any limit the agreement leaves out keeps its Enterprise baseline, and higher caps can be agreed up to the maximum allowed for that metric.
<!-- generated: finite-enterprise-limits:end -->

**Price.** There is no published enterprise price. We work out what your limits cost us to serve, then agree a monthly figure with you.

<!-- generated: finite-enterprise-seats:start -->
**Seats.** Enterprise starts with 25 seats. An agreement can raise that to a set number, up to 250. See [Team & invites](./team.en.md) for how occupied and pending seats are counted.
<!-- generated: finite-enterprise-seats:end -->

**Billing.** Either we invoice you outside the app on your own terms, or we set up a private plan you can pay for in the app like any other subscription.

## Annual contracts

If you contract annually, we record the monthly equivalent (the annual figure divided by twelve) on your agreement. Your invoice still follows whatever schedule we agreed.

## Changing your limits

Ask us. We update the agreement and the new limits apply from the next request. There's no waiting period and no need to re-subscribe.

## If an agreement is paused

An agreement can be suspended, for example while a renewal is being sorted out. While it is suspended your account falls back to the standard Agency-plan allowances rather than losing access. Your data is untouched. When we reactivate the agreement your negotiated limits come straight back.

## Questions

**Can I sign up for an enterprise plan myself?** No. Enterprise limits and pricing are agreed per organization, so an admin switches the agreement on after we've talked.

**Do credit packs still work?** Yes. Packs top up a limit the same way they do on any paid plan.

**What happens if I delete my account?** Your agreement and any enquiries you sent us are deleted with it. See [Your data & privacy](./settings-security.en.md).
