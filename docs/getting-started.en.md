# Getting started

RankMeFast shows how well your site attracts visitors from Google and from AI assistants. Here's how to get from sign-up to your first report.

## 1. Create an account
Sign up with email or Google. If you use email, click the verification link we send you. It expires after 24 hours, so check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived within two minutes.

## 2. Add your site
Enter the full URL, including `https://`. The site has to be public and responding. We keep only the scheme and host, so any path or query string is dropped. Local addresses such as `localhost` and IP addresses are rejected.

## 3. Run your first audit
Click **Run your first audit**. It takes one to three minutes: we crawl a small set of your pages, check technical signals and measure page performance. Paid plans have the monthly limits listed in [Plans, limits & credits](./plans-limits-credits.en.md).

## 4. Read the three tabs
- **Fix now**: the problems Google reacts to fastest. Start here.
- **Watch**: smaller issues, or checks we don't have enough data for yet.
- **Passed**: everything your site cleared.

Each row tells you why the problem matters, which page it's on and how to fix it. For the full walkthrough, see [Reading your audit report](./audit-report.en.md).

## 5. Recommended next steps
- Connect Google Search Console so RankMeFast can tell whether your pages are indexed and whether rich results show up. See [Connecting Google Search Console](./google-search-console.en.md).
- Add a few keywords to track from the site view, so you can follow their positions over time. See [Rank tracking](./rank-tracking.en.md).
