# App Store Optimization

RankMeFast keeps app keyword and chart observations beside store research, review analysis, and a comparison of profiles you paired yourself.

## What it does

Register an app profile for Google Play, the App Store, or both. You can track keyword positions, follow category charts, research store keywords and competitors, analyze a capped sample of reviews, and compare stored observations across a paired profile.

Every result keeps its store, market, language, and observation time. When the source does not return a value, the product says **not observed** instead of filling the gap.

## Tracking and research

Keyword and chart trackers run once a week. Saved history remains available as a chart and an equivalent table, including checks where no position was observed.

Keyword and competitor research run when you request them. Keyword-gap research currently accepts only the United States location and English language; requests for other markets are rejected rather than quietly switched.

## Costs and allowances

The App SEO add-on is available to Pro and Agency accounts for $29 per month. It adds monthly allowances across keyword checks, listing audits, chart checks, research, competitor discovery, and review analysis. Four one-time $19 packs add keyword checks, research pages, competitor runs, or review runs.

See [Plans, limits, and credits](./plans-limits-credits.en.md) for the current quantities, eligibility rules, and what each unit counts.

## Limits

A recorded keyword or chart position does not guarantee a ranking, a store feature, or more installs. Review themes describe only the reviews that run analyzed.

Weekly tracking is not continuous monitoring. Changes between two scheduled checks are not observed, and review analysis runs only when requested.

If an operator turns off part of App SEO, you can't start new work there, but stored results stay readable.
