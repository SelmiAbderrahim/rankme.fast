# Local SEO

If customers find you by searching for a place ("plumber near me", "coffee shop downtown"), most of that traffic comes from map results and business listings rather than the regular blue links. Local SEO tracks the three things that decide whether you show up there. It's available on **Pro** and **Agency**.

## Listing accuracy

Search engines trust a business whose details match everywhere they appear. RankMeFast checks that your **business name, address and phone number** are the same across the listings we can see. Mismatches, like an old address, a second phone number or a shortened name, can push you down in local results without you noticing. We flag each one so you can fix it where it's listed.

## Reviews and questions

We track how many **reviews** you have, your **average rating** and how recent the reviews are, plus the public **questions and answers** on your listing. A steady flow of recent, answered reviews is one of the strongest local signals. If reviews stall or a run of low ratings comes in, you'll see it here early.

## Local map rank

For the keywords you already track, we also record where you appear in the **local map results**, the small group of businesses shown above the regular results. This is separate from your normal search position: a site can rank well in ordinary results and still be missing from the map.

## What one check costs

One **local check** refreshes your listing details, reviews and questions together, and counts as a single unit against your plan. Map-rank checks aren't charged separately; they use your normal rank-check budget. When your monthly local checks run out, they pause until the next reset, or you can top up with credits.
